1Business and geography
Where do we need Azure presence?
  • Markets and users
  • Datacenter and partner locations
  • Planned launches or migrations
  • Anticipated growth and proximity
  • Geographic concentration risk
Decision

Confirm sufficient business value to justify deeper assessment.

2Data and compliance
Are we allowed to operate here?
  • Data residency and sovereignty
  • Regulatory and industry requirements
  • Company policy and customer commitments
  • Support access and encryption
  • Restrictions on data movement or operational control
Decision

Exclude the region when it cannot satisfy a mandatory requirement.

From the workloads
  • Existing and future workloads, including moves
  • Closest-fitting Workload Landing Zone archetype
  • Data, connectivity, and shared services
  • Security, resiliency, and scale
  • Required Azure services
Required from the platform
Platform enablementServices needed locally, remotely, or centrally

Identify and record the workload-driven platform requirements.

4Regional capability and availability
Which regions can support the identified workload requirements?
  • Services, models, and SKUs
  • Availability zones and pairing
  • Latency, pricing, and scale
  • Regional access and quota
  • Service-specific cross-region behavior
Decision

Qualify candidates, documenting conditions, constraints, and unresolved validations.

Qualification outcomeEach regional option is classified against defined workload requirements, with evidence.
ExcludedA mandatory requirement cannot be satisfied.
CandidatePotentially viable; more discovery or validation first.
ConditionalSupported if documented conditions are resolved.
QualifiedAs of the assessment date; onboarding revalidation still required.
