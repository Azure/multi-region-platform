Why this creates agility

Region becomes a deployment parameter

Strategic hubs remain deliberate choices. Workloads stop inheriting those choices automatically.

Today
Region as a permanent architectural dependency

Every workload inherits the same regional pair and its full platform footprint, whether or not its requirements need it.

region = "primary" // fixed for every workload
With this model
Region as a deployment parameter

Each workload selects from qualified regional options that fit its archetype and requirements, validated at decision time.

region = qualified_option(archetype, requirements)
When circumstances change
Capacity is available somewhere else

A qualified region with the required SKUs and quota can take the next deployment.

A required service is stronger in another region

AI models, services, or zone support can favor a different qualified option.

The regulatory or energy environment changes

Placement can adapt within the governed geography instead of waiting for a redesign.

More options for where the next workload can go

Existing workloads can stay put; new and movable ones use the best qualified option available.
