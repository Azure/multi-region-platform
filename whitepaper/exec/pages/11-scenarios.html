Agility in practice

Four scenarios at a glance

From a business or technical driver to a governed regional and workload decision. Illustrative, not customer-specific recommendations.

1
Regulated bank adopts a new in-country regionA new in-country region opens while regulation requires certain data or services to remain there.
QualificationQualified for defined Hybrid-Connected requirements; other workloads remain candidates.
ConnectivityMinimal Regional Hub; existing hybrid connectivity reused where it permits.
PlacementIn-scope workloads relocate selectively; others remain in place.
Qualify for defined requirements without forcing portfolio-wide relocation; if the new region is nonpaired, design the recovery path explicitly.
2
AI team requires a specific model or GPU SKUThe model, SKU, or service is unavailable — or quota-limited — in existing regions.
QualificationQualified for the defined AI requirements; conditional where private connectivity is not yet designed.
ConnectivityDisconnected Spokes where self-contained; connected only where dependencies justify it.
PlacementNet-new deployment, revalidated at decision time.
Do not introduce enterprise connectivity or regional platform capabilities before a workload requirement justifies them.
3
Acquisition brings an estate in another geographyInterdependent applications, shared data, and local users where there is little Azure presence.
QualificationCandidate until dependencies are understood; then qualified for defined portfolio requirements.
ConnectivityRemote Hub Connected, or Minimal Regional Hub where east-west traffic or independence justify it.
PlacementRemain in place initially; relocate selectively as integration proceeds.
Aggregate portfolio dependencies determine the profile; application count or acquisition status does not.
4
Datacenter exit accelerates migrationHybrid-connected applications must leave a colocation facility; is another region now required?
QualificationExisting target region remains qualified; an additional region stays a candidate.
ConnectivityNo new profile: the existing strategic hub is reused; no new hub solely because another region is available.
PlacementMost workloads land in the existing qualified region.
Remaining in an existing qualified region is a valid outcome; a new region can stay an option, not a project.
