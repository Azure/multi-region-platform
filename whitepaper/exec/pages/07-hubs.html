04 · Tier the regional footprint

Pay for local capability only where it is justified

How the profiles trade local capability for platform scope
FigureA qualitative trade-off, not a price list. More local capability usually raises fixed cost and operational scope, but can reduce cross-region dependency, latency exposure, and availability coupling.

There is no required progression between profiles. Select the most efficient profile for current requirements; different profiles coexist across the estate under the same landing-zone guardrails.
