Tier the regional footprint

Not every region needs a full landing zone

A few strategic hubs carry the full stack; other regions use the most efficient pattern that meets their requirements.

With a geographic foundation in place, the full landing zone does not have to be copied into every Azure region. Maintain a small number of strategic geographic hubs — often two or three regions — with the full networking, security, management, and shared-service footprint.

Other regions are consumed through lighter-weight patterns appropriate to each workload, evaluated application by application. The four connectivity profiles are not maturity stages: adoption growth alone does not justify moving toward a Full Regional Hub.
