Compare regional options, record qualification evidence, and select the regional design. The planner supports decisions; it does not qualify a workload automatically or guarantee deployment capacity.

Compare and qualify

Scope current and candidate regions. Compare Services, Latency, and Pricing, then record evidence against the four Checks.

Service, Product, and SKU selectors use an imported availability CSV. Pricing uses a separate Retail Prices API snapshot and service-level comparisons.

The geography view compares region, peering-location, and customer-city latency. Estimated routes do not prove that circuits exist.

Record the design

Regional design records the connectivity profile, shared-service placement, and hybrid connectivity for each selected region.

Hub Connections accepts multiple regions. Existing connectivity can use peering-location suggestions or your own circuit or VPN description.

Enable Advanced Networking Design Tool to explore the inherited design. Tool-only refinements do not modify the authoritative plan; limitations are called out.

Review and export

Review + export collects the evidence and design decisions. Export Excel, print the plan, or save and reopen a JSON planning record.

Visitor-entered planning data stays in the browser; it is not sent to a server. Reference data comes from published snapshots.
